VICE HEAT: Leonida Cover Studio turns any photo into a GTA 6-style cover art poster, right in the browser, fully offline.

- **4 layouts:** 9-panel cover grid (like the official GTA 6 cover), slanted panels, 4-panel, single hero
- **6 colour grades:** Sunset, Neon Night, Twilight Gold, Coral Heat, Vice Violet, Mono Noir
- **Per-panel photos:** upload your own or pick a sample, drag to crop, scroll to zoom; role tags on every panel
- **Poster furniture:** gradient title, subtitle, tagline, quote bar, LEONIDA seal, billing block, star strip, callouts
- **One-click PNG export in 3 sizes:** 1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1920×1080

## How to use

1. Pick a layout and a colour grade.
2. Click a panel to select it, then pick a sample photo or upload your own; drag inside a panel to crop, scroll to zoom.
3. Edit the title, tagline and quote, then export — one click downloads all three PNG sizes.
