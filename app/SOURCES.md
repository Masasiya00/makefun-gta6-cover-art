## How the samples are used

The nine photos are the app's defaults: the 9-panel grid fills one panel
per photo, and Shuffle re-deals them. A user can replace any panel with
their own photo from their machine (drag to crop, scroll to zoom); their
photo never leaves the browser. Every sample first gets the same uniform
pre-grade — saturate +28%, contrast +12%, warm red lift — and then the
grade the user picked.
