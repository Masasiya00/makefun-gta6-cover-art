# Photo sources — VICE HEAT sample art

Every sample photo bundled in `samples/` is a real photograph of real people,
freely licensed for reuse. No Rockstar artwork, logo, or font is used anywhere.
All seven replaced photos were pre-graded in the repo (warm Vice City sunset:
saturation +28%, contrast +12%, warm red lift) so the cover reads as one
graded set; the app's six colour grades then tint them further at render time.

## Notes

- **Unsplash photos** are covered by the Unsplash License
  (https://unsplash.com/license): free to use for commercial and non-commercial
  purposes without permission or attribution.
- **CC photos** (skyline, heli, gator) are used under their Creative Commons
  licences above; attribution is given in this file and the creators are named.
  The CC BY-SA 4.0 skyline photo is shared under the same licence terms.
- **Boulevard / Pier:** these two photos were vendored as free starter samples
  in the first build (10:15 WIB, 20 Nov 2026) and kept unchanged in this fix
  round because they were already the good panels. Their original source URLs
  were not recorded in that round — if a source URL is needed, it still has to
  be traced; nothing about them was invented here.
- User-uploaded photos are the user's own and are never bundled or uploaded.
