## A note on Boulevard & The Pier

Boulevard and The Pier were vendored in the first build round as free
starter samples, and their original source was not recorded at the time.
Their original source URLs are still untraced — if a source URL is needed,
it still has to be traced; nothing about them was invented here. Both are
photographs of real public places, used as placeholder samples; any user can
replace them with their own photos via the per-panel upload button.
